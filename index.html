Method

Recording and verification rules

Cyprus Labor Watch maps strikes, workplace deaths, action calls, and labor-related arrests in one tracker. One dot is one mapped location instance; one case can have multiple locations.

Strike records distinguish decision taken, ongoing, ended, and postponed / banned. Action calls are separated from strikes and become held after their event date.

Sources are prioritized by strength: official/public bodies, unions, established news, and then social media only as a lead requiring corroboration.
